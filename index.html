WHY WE EXIST

Nobody taught us this stuff — so we're teaching it now.

Most of us didn't learn how contracts work, what our rights are during a traffic stop, or how to read a lease, until we had to figure it out the hard way. So we built the resource we wished we had: a national network of student-run chapters teaching legal literacy one workshop, podcast episode, and community booth at a time.

— the LegalLead founding team

WHAT WE DO

Six ways we make the law make sense

Workshops

Local, in-person lessons that break down real legal topics — tenant rights, first jobs, ID stops — in plain language.

Booklets & Infographics

Bite-sized guides you can actually finish reading — no jargon, no fine print.

Community Booths

We show up at fairs, libraries, and school events — wherever people already are.

Explainer PDFs

Free, downloadable, no legal degree required.

Chapter Network

Student leaders bringing LegalLead to their own cities and campuses.
