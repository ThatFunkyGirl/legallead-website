Why we exist

Nobody taught us this stuff — so we're teaching it now.

Most of us didn't learn how contracts work, what our rights are during a traffic stop, or how to read a lease, until we had to figure it out the hard way. So we built the resource we wished we had: a national network of student-run chapters teaching legal literacy one workshop, podcast episode, and community booth at a time.

— the LegalLead founding team

About LegalLead

Our story, in short

LegalLead started with one workshop at a local library, taught by a handful of students who were tired of watching their friends get blindsided by leases, contracts, and traffic stops they didn't understand. That one workshop turned into a podcast. The podcast turned into booklets. The booklets turned into a national network.

We're still students. We're still figuring some of this out as we go. But we've learned that legal knowledge doesn't have to be locked behind a law degree — it just has to be explained well.

What we believe

Access over jargon

If a resource needs a dictionary to understand, we haven't done our job.

Community over classroom

We meet people at libraries and fairs, not in lecture halls.

Youth-led, expert-backed

Students run LegalLead. Real legal professionals help us get it right.

Common questions

Do I need experience?

No. We'll train you. What matters more is that you care about the mission.

Is this only for law students?

Not at all — most of our members aren't. LegalLead works because we explain things like regular people, not lawyers.

What if there's no chapter near me?

You can start one, or join our national team remotely.
